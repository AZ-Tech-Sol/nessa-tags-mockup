How it works

Three steps, and the last one takes half a second.

1

Pick your look

Choose a shape and your colours. Butterflies, mushrooms, moons, leaves, or your own name cut right into it.

2

Nessa prints it

Each tag is printed and finished by hand, then the little chip inside is set to point at your link.

3

Tap it to any phone

Hold the tag to the top of a phone and your page opens straight away. Nothing to install, nothing to scan.

How paying works

Everything is handled in person — no card details on this website, and nothing to pay online. This page just tells Nessa what you want.

Batches start with half when you meet up, which buys the filament and puts your order on the printer. The other half is due when your tags are finished and in your hands.

Ready-made: $150 on a 50 · $250 on a 100 · $300 on a 150.
Custom: $200 on a 50 · $350 on a 100 · $450 on a 150.
